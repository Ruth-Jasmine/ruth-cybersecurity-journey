# Certifications

Study notes, schedules, and progress logs for certifications.

## Planned path

Given the 12–14 month consultant goal:

| Cert | Why | When |
|------|-----|------|
| CompTIA Security+ | Foundation; recognized by every employer | Months 1–4 |
| ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) | Often free or low-cost via ISC2 candidate programs — verify current pricing | Months 4–5 |
| CompTIA CySA+ *or* PenTest+ | Defensive or offensive specialization, depending on which consulting flavor appeals | Months 6–10 |
| CISSP (Associate of ISC2) | Long-term; full cert requires 5 years' experience. "Associate" status starts the clock. | Year 2–5 |
| AI security certs | Field is young; track ISC2, SANS, and ISACA as offerings emerge | Year 2+ |

## What goes in this folder

- Per-cert study schedules
- Domain-by-domain notes
- Practice exam scores and weak-area logs
- Post-exam reflections (what mattered, what didn't)

## Budget realism

- Security+ exam: approximately $400 USD (one attempt) — verify current price at comptia.org
- Practice tests (Jason Dion, Pocket Prep, etc.): $30–60
- Course materials: free options (Professor Messer) are excellent; paid (Mike Chapple book, Pluralsight) optional

Plan finances for one potential re-take per cert. Most people pass on the first attempt with serious preparation, but it's not guaranteed.
