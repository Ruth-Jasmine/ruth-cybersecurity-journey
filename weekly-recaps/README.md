# Weekly Recaps

Weekly synthesis of learning, reflections, and progress toward the cybersecurity consultant goal.

## Format

Each entry: `week-NN-short-title.md` (e.g., `week-01-getting-started.md`)

Required sections:

- **What I studied** — topics, hours, key concepts
- **What I built or practiced** — labs, exercises, projects
- **What I struggled with** — specific blockers, not vague frustrations
- **Plan for next week** — 2–4 concrete goals
- **Reflection** — one paragraph; what surprised me, what I want to remember

## Why publish recaps

Three reasons:

1. Forces honest weekly self-assessment
2. Creates a public track record that compounds — recruiters and hiring managers value visible learning trajectories
3. The "learning in public" community responds well to consistent posters; there's a real network effect

## Cadence advice

Thursdays or Fridays tend to work best — far enough into the week to have substance, soon enough to plan the next one. Pick a day and protect it.
