# AI Security

OWASP LLM Top 10, AI governance, adversarial AI, and related notes.

## What lives here

- Notes on OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications
- AI governance frameworks (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001)
- Adversarial ML techniques (prompt injection, jailbreaks, model extraction, data poisoning)
- MLOps and AI supply-chain security
- MITRE ATLAS threat catalog notes

## Suggested learning order

1. **OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications** — start here. Concrete vulnerability categories that map to consulting conversations.
2. **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** (AI 100-1) — four functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage. The governance vocabulary clients and regulators use.
3. **EU AI Act** — risk-based approach (unacceptable / high / limited / minimal). Matters for any client touching the EU market.
4. **MITRE ATLAS** — adversarial threat catalog for ML systems; the AI equivalent of MITRE ATT&CK.
5. **Foundational adversarial ML papers** — see `06-reading-summaries/`.

## Why this is a smart specialization

AI security is a young field. Formal certifications and senior experts are scarce. A consultant who can credibly speak to OWASP LLM Top 10, NIST AI RMF, and the EU AI Act has a real differentiator right now. The field will mature fast — early structured learning compounds.
